# HoHa Slide modifications

HoHa Slide contains substantial modifications to software originally made
available by the Presenton contributors under the Apache License, Version 2.0.

Copyright 2026 HoHa Slide contributors.

## Modification notice

The following areas have been changed or newly implemented by the HoHa Slide
contributors:

- Product name, logos, visual identity, Vietnamese and English user interface.
- Vietnamese-first presentation workflow and student-oriented product design.
- Administration and content-management features.
- Presentation planning, design rules, quality evaluation, and AI orchestration.
- Independent editable PPTX generation based on PptxGenJS.
- Template conversion, validation, and export-runtime synchronization tooling.
- Removal of upstream analytics and upstream-facing product integrations.

Files changed by HoHa Slide are recorded in the project's version-control
history. New and substantially rewritten source files should include an SPDX
identifier and a short `Modified by HoHa Slide contributors` notice where the
file format permits comments.

This notice does not replace the attribution and redistribution requirements
in `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`.

## Distribution rule

Every source or binary distribution of HoHa Slide must include:

1. `LICENSE`;
2. `NOTICE`;
3. this `MODIFICATIONS.md` file; and
4. the applicable third-party license notices produced for that release.

Release owners must regenerate the dependency inventory and review bundled
assets, fonts, templates, model prompts, and executable components before each
commercial release.
